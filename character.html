Who will you swim with?

Pick a companion and follow your curiosity. You can change sharks along the way.

How to play

Hold and drag to swim, or use arrow keys / WASD. Swim into the fluffy white bubbles to discover shark facts. Each bubble becomes a flag—visit the flag to read it again.

Bounce with jellyfish and seals, snack on small fish, and try a spin!
